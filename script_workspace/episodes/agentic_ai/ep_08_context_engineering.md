## The Video Script

### 1. Voiceover Narration Script
This is the clean text you will read during voice recording/teleprompting.

**Riya (Intro Hook at 0.0s - 10.0s):**
"Most people focus on writing the perfect prompt. But that's not enough. Today you'll learn the real skill behind smart AI agents — Context Engineering."

**Presenter:**
"Here's something most people get wrong. They think AI is all about the prompt. Write a better question, get a better answer. Right?

Not really.

Even the best prompt fails if the AI doesn't have the right information. Think about it. You hire a new assistant. Would you just ask them a question on day one? No. You'd give them your files. Your calendar. Your preferences. Then you ask.

That's the difference.

Prompt engineering — better questions.
Context engineering — better information.

So what goes into good context? Four things.

One — the system prompt. This tells the AI who it is. 'You're a technical writer. Be precise.' That's the identity.

Two — documents. The AI pulls real files from your knowledge base. Product specs. Meeting notes. Research papers.

Three — tool results. The AI calls your calendar API. Checks the weather. Looks up a database. Live data, right now.

Four — memory. Past conversations. What the user likes. What worked before.

You put all four together? That's a context-engineered agent. That's how a basic chatbot becomes a smart assistant that actually knows what's going on."

**Riya (Question Overlay at 58.0s - 68.0s):**
"This makes sense. But how does the context get better over time? Do we fix it by hand every time something goes wrong?"

**Presenter:**
"No, and that's the best part. We set up a feedback loop. After each response, the system checks — was it good? Was something missing?

If the context was incomplete, the system learns to include that next time. And tools like Google's Vertex AI optimizer can do this automatically. They test different contexts and pick the best one.

So remember — a good prompt is just the start. The real power is in what the AI knows before it answers.

That wraps up Chapter 1! Subscribe to keep building with me!"
