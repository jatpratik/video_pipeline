# Episode 02: The AI Agent Paradigm Shift

- **Target Duration**: 70 seconds
- **Narrator Tone**: Friendly, clear, tech-focused AI educator.
- **Riya's Timing**: 45.0s - 54.0s (9.0s duration).

## The Video Script

### 1. Voiceover Narration Script
This is the clean text you will read during voice recording/teleprompting.

**Presenter:**
"Stop building simple wrapper apps. The way we build AI is changing completely. In just two years, we went from simple chat prompts to smart systems that can work together.

At first, we just wrote simple prompts. Then, we used RAG to give the model real files and documents to read. After that, we made single agents that could use tools. Today, we are in the era of Agentic AI—where a team of specialized agents work together to solve big, complex goals.

And companies are adopting this very fast. Studies show that ninety-six percent of large IT companies are starting to use agents. In fact, AI agent startups have already raised over two billion dollars, and the market is expected to reach nearly two hundred billion dollars by 2034.

We are moving from a single model to a team of agents. But working in a team has its own challenges."

**Riya (Overlay at 45.0s - 54.0s):**
"Wait, but doesn't a team of agents make the system slow and expensive? When is a team actually better than just one good prompt?"

**Presenter:**
"Good question, Riya. For simple tasks, a team is too much. But for complex workflows, a single prompt gets confused and makes mistakes. Having multiple specialized agents lets us divide the work. This makes it much easier to test, fix, and scale the system.

So how complex can these agents actually get? In our next video, we will look at the four levels of AI agents."
