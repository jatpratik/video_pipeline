## 4. Profile Picture (DP) Recommendation
Since you are using your own photo:
- **Background**: Use a solid, bright color (like light blue, orange, or teal) or a clean dark background. This makes your face pop.
- **Lighting**: Make sure your face is bright and clear.
- **Expression**: A friendly smile works best for an educational channel!
