# Episode 01: The Complete AI Engineer Roadmap

## PART 1: THE STUDY GUIDE (For You)

### The Intuition: Core AI vs Generative AI
When people want to learn AI, they often look at huge roadmaps and get scared. They think they need to learn heavy math and coding all at once. 

But actually, to become an AI Engineer, you can choose between two main paths depending on your background and interest:

1. **Track 1: Core AI (Math & Models)**
   - **Who it is for**: Students, or anyone who enjoys mathematics and wants to understand how models are built and trained from scratch.
   - **Focus**: Building and training models, understanding how they work deep inside.
   - **The Path**:
     - **Phase 1: Foundations**: Simple Linear Algebra (matrices, vectors), Calculus (gradients), and basic Python.
     - **Phase 2: Data Handling**: NumPy and Pandas to clean and visualize data.
     - **Phase 3: Machine Learning**: Scikit-learn to build simple models like Decision Trees and Regression.
     - **Phase 4: Deep Learning**: Learning how neural networks work, using PyTorch, and understanding Transformers.
     - **Phase 5: Advanced Topics**: Computer Vision (YOLO) and Natural Language Processing.
     - **Phase 6: MLOps**: Packaging models with Docker and deploying them.

2. **Track 2: Generative AI & Agentic AI (Software & Systems)**
   - **Who it is for**: Software engineers or anyone who already knows programming principles and wants to build AI products quickly.
   - **Focus**: Building intelligent applications, agent workflows, and systems using existing models.
   - **The Path**:
     - **Phase 1: API & Prompting**: How to call LLMs, write good prompts, and get structured outputs.
     - **Phase 2: RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: Connecting LLMs to private databases so they can read documents.
     - **Phase 3: AI Agents**: Giving LLMs tools (like web search or database access) and letting them run in loops (plan, act, check).
     - **Phase 4: Multi-Agent Systems**: Making multiple AI agents talk to each other to solve big tasks.
     - **Phase 5: LLMOps**: Testing, evaluating, and monitoring AI apps in production.
