# Episode 00: Channel Intro & Vision Roadmap

## PART 1: THE STUDY GUIDE (For You)

### The Intuition: Why we need visual learning
Most online videos are either too hard or too simple:
1. **Too much Math**: They show long equations and formulas without explaining the basic idea or how to write code.
2. **Just copying code**: They tell you to copy 5 lines of code to make a chatbot. But they do not explain how it works under the hood.

To build real AI projects, you need two things: first, a clear visual understanding of the math, and second, knowing how to write the code. This channel will use simple 3D animations to make hard math and AI concepts easy to see and understand.

### The Two Tracks
We will learn AI through two tracks:

1. **Track 1: Fundamental Maths for AI**
   - **Focus**: Understanding the math behind AI using simple visuals.
   - **Key Concepts**: Vectors, matrices, how neural networks learn, and how math helps models understand data.
   
2. **Track 2: Generative AI & Agentic AI**
   - **Focus**: Building smart AI applications and agents.
   - **Key Concepts**: Large Language Models, prompt engineering, AI agents that can think and take action, and connecting multiple AI agents together.
