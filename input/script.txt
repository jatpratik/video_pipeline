Want to know why most AI agents break in production?
Because they trust raw text.
 
Here’s the hidden problem with prompt chaining.
Even if your prompts are good…
The output between steps is usually messy.
One model says:
“Here are the trends…”
Another says:
“I found three important insights…”
Another changes the format completely.
And suddenly your entire pipeline breaks.
Because AI systems don’t fail from intelligence problems.
They fail from formatting problems.
That’s why production AI systems use structured outputs.
Usually JSON.
Instead of vague paragraphs…
The model returns clean machine-readable data.
 
Like this
 
Now the next step knows EXACTLY where the data is.
No guessing.
No parsing chaos.
No broken chains.
This is what turns prompt chaining into actual engineering.
And honestly…
Most beginner AI apps completely ignore this layer.
 
That happens all the time.
That’s why modern agent systems add validators, retries, and schema enforcement before moving to the next step.
Production agents don’t just trust the model blindly.
They verify everything.
And once you can trust structured outputs…
You unlock something way bigger—
AI systems that can call tools, APIs, and databases reliably.
