Most AI systems fail for one simple reason… they try to do EVERYTHING in one prompt.

You know what most beginners do with LLMs?
They write one massive prompt.
“Analyze this report, find trends, summarize everything, write an email, and make it professional.”
And then the model completely falls apart.
Because LLMs are terrible at handling too many cognitive tasks at once.
They forget instructions.
Lose context.
Hallucinate details.
Or skip important parts entirely.
That’s why real AI systems use something called Prompt Chaining.
Instead of one giant prompt…
You split the task into smaller focused steps.
First prompt: summarize the report.
Second prompt: extract trends from the summary.
Third prompt: generate the email.
Now every step has ONE job.
And here’s the important part—
The output from one step becomes the input for the next step.
So the AI builds reasoning progressively… like a pipeline.
This is basically how modern AI agents work internally.
Not one brain explosion.
More like coordinated mini-thoughts.

That’s exactly why production systems use validation and structured outputs like JSON between steps.
Because one messy output can break the entire pipeline.
Good agent systems don’t just chain prompts…
They control the data flowing between them.

And that structured-output layer?
That’s actually what separates toy AI demos from real production-grade agents.
